# Børgefjell 2025 inventory

Migrated from [Børgefjell 2025](https://turer.kvalvaag.org/posts/beinvatna_2025/) (published 2025-09-20). The source folder is `beinvatna_2025`. The page lists the GPX files once for the whole article, not under a day heading.

The three tracks are foot tracks on 15, 16 and 17 September, each starting at camp around 08:00. Dag 1 says the group met at Værnes at 12:00 and then drove, so that track is not the drive. Dag 1 is stored on 14 September, the day before the first morning at camp. That date is inferred: the article does not name it. Dag 2–4 follow the three track dates in order. The texts all mention Beinvatna and Getsen, and the tracks were not distinct enough to swap those three days.
